About QONE

QONE Corporation PE-foam manufacturer in Yeoju, Korea.

QONE Corporation (주식회사 큐원) is a Korean manufacturer of PE foam interior and living products, founded on 6 January 2011. At its own factory in Yeoju, Gyeonggi-do, it makes CUSHIONI silk wallpaper, insulation wallpaper, 3D foam blocks, herringbone living mats and folding camping mats, from foam sheet to finished product, and it takes OEM and private-label work. QONE supplies Costco in 5 countries (Japan, China, Korea, Taiwan and Australia) and exports to 25+ countries. A separate sourcing range, designed in Korea and made in China, is listed apart from QONE's own products.
