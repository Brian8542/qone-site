About QONE

QONE Corporation PE-foam manufacturer in Yeoju, Korea.

QONE Corporation (주식회사 큐원) is a PE-foam manufacturer with its own factory in Yeoju, Gyeonggi-do, Korea. The company was founded on 6 January 2011. It produces CUSHIONI foam blocks, insulation wallpaper, camping mats and living mats, and it also takes OEM and private-label work. A separate sourcing range in the catalog is designed in Korea and manufactured in China. QONE supplies Costco Korea and exports.
