Silk Wallpaper

Silk Wallpaper

A soft, premium silk-texture wallpaper with grid embossing, in seven colors, that installs like a sticker.

A soft, premium-feeling silk-texture wallpaper finished with a grid embossing pattern. The peel-and-stick backing means anyone can install it without tools, and the range of seven colors — from Snow White to Space Gray — makes it easy to match any room's palette.

Key features

  • Soft, premium silk finish
  • Grid embossing
  • Peel-and-stick, tool-free

Specifications

Size50 × 250 cm
ColorsSnow White, Petal Pink, Boy Blue, Lime Green, Sand Beige, Warm Gray, Space Gray
MaterialPolyethylene, acrylic adhesive
OriginMade in Korea

Frequently asked questions

How is it installed?

Peel off the backing and press it onto a clean, dry interior wall. No paste or tools are needed.

What size is one sheet?

50 × 250 cm. A 70 × 250 cm vertical-embossed version and a 50 × 1000 cm bulk roll are also available.

Is it the same as CUSHIONI insulation wallpaper?

No. Silk wallpaper is a decorative finish. Insulation wallpaper is a separate 8-layer PE foam product for cold walls.

Buyer questions

Is there a minimum order?

MOQ depends on the product, size and printing. Tell us the quantity you have in mind and we will confirm the MOQ for your order.

Which certifications does QONE hold?

QONE holds ISO 9001, ISO 14001, Main-Biz and Brand K. KATRI testing did not detect 6 heavy metals. The factory has passed a major warehouse-club COC/LRQA audit and Daiso Japan WCA audits.

How long does shipping take?

Standard items usually ship 30–45 days after order confirmation, from Busan or Incheon. FOB, CIF and other Incoterms are agreed per order.

Do you offer OEM or private label?

Yes. Design, tooling, production and packaging are handled in-house.
