Four advantages of folding foam mats

Puncture-proofNo air inside — nothing to leak, nothing to patch.
Ready in secondsAccordion folds: unfold to set up, fold to pack. No pump.
Shrugs off water and dirtThe surface sheds water and wipes clean, and it blocks ground moisture.
Insulates from the groundClosed-cell foam slows the cold coming up from the earth.

Foam vs. air mats

Folding PE foamAir mat

Want simple and reliable? Go foam. Want maximum cushioning? Go air — or layer an air mat on top of a foam mat for the best of both.

Choosing the right mat

1. People and size

Solo mats for one person; mid-size to large (such as 1500 × 2000 mm) for families and picnics. Check your tent floor size.

2. Folds and portability

2- and 3-fold mats open quickly; 4- and 8-fold mats pack small and light. Roll mats suit hiking and touring.

3. How you will use it

  • Car camping — a flat, thicker mat levels out seat folds and trunk bumps.
  • Solo camping & hiking — light and compact.
  • Picnics & sports days — quick to unfold and easy to wipe.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use it for car camping?

Yes. It lies flat, so it smooths out seat gaps and a cold trunk floor. Choose a size that fits your vehicle.

Does it work on damp ground?

PE foam barely absorbs water and blocks ground moisture. Wipe it dry and let it air out before packing.

How do I clean it?

Wipe with water or a cloth with diluted mild detergent and dry in the shade. Long exposure to direct sun or a hot car can deform it.
