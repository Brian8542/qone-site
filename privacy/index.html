Privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026

QONE Corporation (주식회사 큐원) runs www.qone.kr. This page explains what the inquiry forms collect and how that information is used. The contact address is chl@qone.kr.

1. What the inquiry forms collect

You type these details yourself. Which fields appear depends on the form, and only the fields you fill in are sent.

Details you may enterName, company name, email address, country or destination country, product, product details, estimated quantity, sales channel, website, and your message.
How it is sentThe form is submitted to FormSubmit at formsubmit.co, which delivers the message by email to chl@qone.kr.

2. Why we use it

  • To reply to a product, wholesale, sample, quote, or OEM inquiry.
  • To keep in touch about that inquiry.

3. How long we keep it

Messages are kept while we are answering the inquiry and, if a business relationship follows, while that relationship continues. We delete them when they are no longer needed, unless a law that applies to QONE Corporation requires a longer period.

4. Who else handles the message

QONE Corporation does not sell these details. FormSubmit processes the submission only to deliver it to chl@qone.kr. The factory address is 166 Yeonha-dong-ro, Yeoju-si, Gyeonggi-do 12646, Korea.

5. Google Analytics

Pages load Google Analytics (gtag.js, measurement ID G-3WS8ZB6PX8) after you use the page or a few seconds after it loads. Google Analytics may store a cookie to count visits. The inquiry form does not set its own cookie.

6. Your request

To ask what we hold, or to correct or delete it, email chl@qone.kr.
