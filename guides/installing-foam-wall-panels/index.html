How to Install Peel-and-Stick 3D Foam Wall Panels

QONE's 3D foam wall panels are designed for simple, tool-free self-installation. This is a general step-by-step overview — always check the instructions included with your specific product, and contact us if you have questions about a particular pattern or size.

  1. Prepare the wall surface

    Clean the wall so it is free of dust, grease and loose paint. The surface should be dry and as smooth as possible for the adhesive to bond well.

  2. Measure and plan the layout

    Measure the wall and dry-fit a few panels first to plan how the pattern will line up, especially at corners and edges.

  3. Peel the backing and apply

    Peel away the protective backing from one panel at a time and press it onto the wall starting from a corner or a level reference line.

  4. Press and smooth

    Press firmly across the whole panel, working from the center outward, to remove air pockets and ensure full contact with the wall.

  5. Trim if needed

    At edges, corners or around outlets, trim the panel carefully to fit using a sharp utility knife.
