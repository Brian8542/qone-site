Why work with QONE

  • Factory direct — no trading-company margin; your spec questions reach the people who make the product.
  • One integrated plant — from raw foam sheet to finished goods, quality is owned in one place.
  • OEM & private label — your patterns, sizes, colors and packaging; design and tooling in-house. (OEM details)
  • Audit-proven — global-retailer factory audits (CoC/LRQA), Daiso Japan WCA; ISO 9001 and ISO 14001.
  • Fast communication — WhatsApp, email and phone with our export director.

How an order works

  1. Inquiry — tell us the product, volume and sales channel via WhatsApp, email or the form.
  2. Samples — we review your needs and send samples by international courier.
  3. Quotation — priced by quantity, specification and packaging.
  4. Order & production — standard items usually ship 30–45 days after confirmation (varies by volume and season); OEM development time is quoted separately.
  5. Shipping — from Busan or Incheon on FOB, CIF or other agreed Incoterms; export documents handled in-house.

Frequently asked questions

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQ depends on the product, size and printing. Share your target quantity and channel and we will confirm what is possible.

Can I get samples?

Yes — samples are available for business buyers. Tell us your company, sales channel and products of interest.

What are your payment terms?

For container orders, T/T and L/C at sight are typical. Terms are agreed per order.

How long is production?

Standard items usually ship 30–45 days after order confirmation, depending on volume and season. OEM projects add development time.

Where do you ship from?

Busan and Incheon, Korea, on FOB, CIF or other agreed Incoterms. We prepare the export documents.

Talk to the factory directly.

WhatsApp or email (chl@qone.kr). Samples, quotes, wholesale and OEM — usually answered within 24 hours.
