Questions buyers ask

Can I order the same items that were sold at Daiso?

Yes. The designs, materials and specifications are available to you. Products are packed in your own label or in neutral packaging; packaging that carries another retailer's brand is not used.

What is the minimum order?

Minimums are set per item and confirmed with the quotation. Each product page shows its pack unit and carton size so you can plan an assortment.

How do you quote prices?

We quote FOB per item and quantity after a mutual NDA. Send your target price point and we will shortlist items that fit it.

Can you change colors, prints or packaging?

For volume orders, colors, prints, pack counts and packaging can be adjusted. Tell us what your shelves need.

Do you ship to my country?

We work with buyers worldwide. Some destinations need extra checks on payment routes and sanctions before we can quote.

Who is QONE?

QONE Corporation is a Korean manufacturer and exporter based in Yeoju, Gyeonggi-do, founded in 2011. For this range we are the export partner and your single point of contact for the Yiwu manufacturer.
