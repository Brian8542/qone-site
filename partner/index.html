Vendor onboarding, eight steps

The order can change by country, buyer and category. An audit may come before the proposal, and samples may go back and forth. There is no promised total timeline.

01

Inquiry received

Send company and product files. QONE replies by email within one business day.

02

Internal review

Fit, capacity and certificates. Outcome: proceed, proceed after changes, or hold. No prices are published here.

03

Samples and club pack

Check samples. Draft a club-size pack, PDQ and band.

04

Buyer proposal

Confirm the spec and present it. The buyer decides.

05

Factory assessment

The factory may be audited. QONE briefs the preparation and any CAP. Order can vary.

06

Order and item

Quantity, date, labels, barcode and GTIN. The item number is assigned by the buyer.

07

Make, inspect, ship

The company produces. QONE inspects (including AQL), pallets, export documents and the container.

08

Reorder

Share feedback. A reorder is discussed, not promised.

Yeoju export base

QONE Corporation was founded on 6 January 2011. The factory is at 166 Yeonha-dong-ro, Yeoju, about 6,000 pyeong (19,834 m²). The Seoul office is Center Place 2312, 40 Namdaemun-ro 9-gil, Jung-gu.

PE foam is foamed, laminated, printed, cut and packed in one plant, with in-house molds. Several containers can be loaded at once. Shipments leave Busan or Incheon on FOB, CIF or another agreed term. Commercial invoice, packing list and certificate of origin are prepared in-house. Partner goods can be received and checked at Yeoju before loading, or shipped another way if the item and the date require it.

Published qualifications include ISO 9001, ISO 14001, Main-Biz, Brand K, and a KATRI test with six heavy metals not detected. Audit scores are not published.

FAQ

How long does entry take?

There is no promised end-to-end time. It depends on the product, certificates, the buyer’s calendar and the season. QONE shares a date at each step.

Can you supply a product you do not make?

Yes. In September 2025 a six-piece travel-cube set was sourced, pallet-packed and shipped for Australia. Each item is reviewed.

Can we keep our brand?

Buy-and-supply is the default. OEM, ODM, private label, and export support that keeps your brand are also used.

Is a factory audit required?

It depends on the country, buyer and category. The producing factory may be the site. In 2026 QONE completed CAP after a GMP/FQA factory assessment and passed an LRQA CoC audit. Scores are not published.

May we propose several items?

Yes. In 2026 intake is item by item, with no category limit.

What if we are not listed?

A listing is not guaranteed. The buyer decides. QONE is not a subsidiary or agent of the club.

Send the product file.

Introduction, photos, size and weight, certificates, and monthly capacity. We reply by email within one business day.
